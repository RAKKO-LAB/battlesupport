Overlay Damage Calculator

A support tool that overlays a translucent panel on your battle game screen for damage calculation, matchup analysis and battle records.

Get it on Google Play User guide

For Android / Available on Google Play

Features

Calculate, check and log without switching apps, right on top of the game screen.

Available in 8 languages

You can change the in-app language at any time. Chinese is offered in two scripts, Simplified and Traditional.

Register your team

Register your team by capturing the screen, or by importing and exporting in Showdown format.

How your data is handled

Your teams and battle records are stored on your device. Captured screen images are never sent off the device.

For better analysis, there is a setting to send anonymous battle data (results and picks), which you can turn off at any time. We do not collect your name, email address, account information or advertising ID. See the privacy policy for details.
